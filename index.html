About The Daily Compass

The Daily Compass｜每日罗盘 is a bilingual archive for reading global news through language, culture, technology, gender, and power. It is designed for slow reading, vocabulary building, and daily reflection.

《每日罗盘》是一份双语全球资讯小报，用语言、文化、技术、性别与权力的线索来阅读世界。它适合慢读、积累表达、练习英文，也适合保存每天的思考。
