# Why Is the Tradwife Fantasy So Seductive?

# 为什么“传统妻子”的幻想如此迷人？

> **Editorial review draft｜编辑审核稿**
> 本稿依据截至 2026 年 8 月 15 日已核验的新闻、学术研究、官方数据与历史资料撰写。中英内容按段落对照。文中 Tradwife 保留英文标签，以避免将其与一般家庭主妇、全职母亲或家庭照护者混为一谈。
>
> This draft is based on journalism, academic research, official data, and historical materials verified through August 15, 2026. Chinese and English are paired paragraph by paragraph. The label “Tradwife” is retained to avoid conflating it with homemakers, stay-at-home mothers, or family caregivers in general.

## 01 — Research Question｜研究问题

打开社交媒体，一种关于家庭生活的画面正在反复出现：长裙、刚出炉的面包、整洁的厨房、成群的孩子，以及仿佛不受通勤、会议和电子邮件打扰的缓慢时间。在这些影像里，家庭不再是现代生活中又一处需要赶工的现场，而像是一条回到稳定、秩序与意义的路。

Open social media and a recurring vision of domestic life appears: long dresses, bread fresh from the oven, immaculate kitchens, many children, and a sense of time apparently untouched by commutes, meetings, or email. In these images, the home is no longer another site of rushed modern labor. It looks like a path back to stability, order, and meaning.

这种内容常被称为 Tradwife——“traditional wife”的缩写。但这个标签从来不是一个边界清楚的群体。有人主动用它描述以丈夫供养、妻子照护为理想的婚姻；有人只分享复古穿搭、烘焙或农场生活；还有一些创作者被媒体和观众贴上标签，自己却并不认同。家庭主妇是一种劳动与家庭角色，Tradwife 美学是一套视觉语言，Tradwife 身份是主动的自我命名，Tradwife 意识形态则把传统异性恋分工呈现为应当遵循的秩序。它们可能重叠，却不能互换。

This content is often called “Tradwife,” short for “traditional wife.” Yet the label has never described a neatly bounded group. Some women use it for a marriage ideal in which the husband provides and the wife cares for the home. Others share vintage clothing, baking, or farm life. Still others are labeled Tradwives by audiences and the media without adopting the identity themselves. A homemaker is a labor and family role; the Tradwife aesthetic is a visual language; Tradwife identity is a form of self-identification; and Tradwife ideology presents a traditional heterosexual division of roles as a normative order. These categories can overlap, but they are not interchangeable.

因此，本期不打算简单回答“Tradwife 是什么”，也不预设它等于极右翼或等于女性自由选择。我们想追问的是：**当 Tradwife 被包装成一种生活方式、一种个人选择和一条逃离现代压力的路径时，它究竟在向女性出售什么？为什么这个幻想如此迷人？**

This report does not simply ask what a Tradwife is, nor does it assume that Tradwife means either the far right or women’s free choice. Instead, it asks: **When Tradwife culture is packaged as a lifestyle, a personal choice, and a route out of modern pressure, what exactly is it selling to women? Why is the fantasy so seductive?**

一个可供检验的答案是：Tradwife 文化把结构性的疲惫，转化成了一场关于“回归”的个人幻想。它回应了真实的过劳、照护危机与工作—家庭冲突，却常把解决方案放在私人家庭内部：找到一个能够供养家庭的丈夫，退出职场，恢复明确的性别角色。现有证据支持这个答案的一部分，但也要求我们限制它：不是所有观看者都相信传统性别秩序，不是所有创作者都属于极右翼，平台上的审美接触也不等于自动的政治激进化。

One answer worth testing is that Tradwife culture turns structural exhaustion into an individualized fantasy of return. It responds to genuine overwork, failures of care, and work-family conflict, yet often locates the solution inside the private household: find a husband able to provide, leave paid employment, and restore clearly divided gender roles. The evidence supports part of this answer, but also requires limits. Not every viewer believes in traditional gender hierarchy; not every creator belongs to the far right; and aesthetic exposure on a platform is not the same as automatic political radicalization.

## 02 — Research｜研究

### Fantasy｜幻想

Tradwife 内容的第一重力量不是政策主张，而是感觉。面团在木桌上发酵，阳光落在亚麻布和铜锅上，母亲从容地为孩子准备食物。画面有意压低现代生活的噪声，把家庭呈现为可以掌控的小世界。它出售的不只是某一种婚姻，更是一种情绪上的确定性：生活有节奏，劳动有结果，每个人似乎都有清楚的位置。

The first power of Tradwife content is not a policy argument but a feeling. Dough rises on a wooden table; sunlight falls across linen and copper pans; a mother calmly prepares food for her children. The frame lowers the volume of modern life and presents the home as a small, controllable world. What it sells is not only a form of marriage, but emotional certainty: life has a rhythm, labor produces visible results, and everyone seems to have a clear place.

Kate Scott 与 Lindsay Day 对 TikTok 上 61 名自我呈现为 Tradwife 的创作者所发布的 102 条帖子进行了质性内容分析。她们发现，传统女性气质、宗教服从、母职、生殖未来想象和殖民怀旧，常通过图像、声音与语言共同出现。研究者将 85.2% 的样本创作者编码为白人，26% 的视频出现小型农场。这个并非随机抽取的样本不能代表整个 TikTok，更不能证明观众会接受其中的价值观；它能够说明的是，在一组明确的 Tradwife 内容中，家庭生活如何被制作成看似自然、普遍而宁静的理想。

Kate Scott and Lindsay Day conducted a qualitative content analysis of 102 TikTok posts from 61 creators who presented themselves as Tradwives. They found that traditional femininity, religious submission, motherhood, reproductive futurity, and colonial nostalgia often appeared together through image, sound, and language. The researchers coded 85.2 percent of creators in the sample as white, while 26 percent of the videos featured small hobby farms. This non-random sample cannot represent all of TikTok or prove that viewers adopt its values. What it can show is how a defined set of Tradwife posts produces domestic life as a seemingly natural, universal, and peaceful ideal.

这种吸引力之所以有效，也因为它没有凭空制造现代生活的不满。美国劳工统计局 2024 年时间使用调查显示，在有 6 岁以下儿童的家庭中，女性平均每天用于主要育儿活动 3 小时，男性为 2 小时。时间使用数据不能告诉我们谁想成为 Tradwife，也不能解释每个家庭如何分工；它至少提醒我们，照护负担仍然是不均等的现实，而“离开这一切”的愿望可能来自真正的疲惫。

The fantasy resonates because it does not invent dissatisfaction with modern life from nothing. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics’ 2024 American Time Use Survey found that, among adults living with children under six, women spent an average of three hours a day on primary childcare activities, compared with two hours for men. Time-use data cannot tell us who wants to become a Tradwife or explain the arrangements of every household. It does remind us that care remains unevenly distributed—and that the wish to “leave it all” may begin in real exhaustion.

### Escape｜逃离

过去十多年，女性被反复要求“拥有一切”：职业成功、经济独立、体面的亲密关系、投入的母职，以及始终可展示的自我管理。Tradwife 幻想并不否认这些压力，而是提供一个看似干净的出口。它不再要求女性同时成为理想员工与理想母亲，而是把“退出”描述为找回女性气质、家庭价值和内心平静。

For more than a decade, women have repeatedly been told to “have it all”: professional success, economic independence, a fulfilling relationship, intensive motherhood, and a constantly presentable self. The Tradwife fantasy does not deny these pressures. It offers what looks like a clean exit. Rather than asking women to be both ideal workers and ideal mothers, it presents withdrawal as a recovery of femininity, family values, and inner peace.

Sarah Banet-Weiser 与 Sara Reinis 分析了 Instagram 和 TikTok 上 50 个 Tradwife 账号。她们指出，Tradwife 话语与女性主义批评其实触碰了三项共同的物质问题：资本主义的 hustle culture、生殖与照护劳动被贬值，以及照护体系失灵。分歧不在于问题是否存在，而在于责任被放在哪里。部分 Tradwife 内容把女性的疲惫归因于女性主义让她们离开家庭，并把回到传统秩序当作私人解决方案。

Sarah Banet-Weiser and Sara Reinis analyzed 50 Tradwife accounts on Instagram and TikTok. They argue that Tradwife discourse and feminist critique encounter three shared material problems: capitalist hustle culture, the devaluation of reproductive and care labor, and failing systems of care. The disagreement is not over whether these problems exist, but where responsibility is placed. Some Tradwife content attributes women’s exhaustion to feminism for drawing them away from the home, then presents a return to traditional order as a private solution.

这解释了幻想的政治暧昧性。一个人真诚地想离开有害职场、更多照顾孩子，或依照宗教信仰安排家庭生活，并不自动构成反女权政治。可是，当缺少托育、低工资、超长工时和家庭内不平等被重新讲述为“女人选错了位置”，公共问题便被改写成个人失败；只有拥有稳定伴侣收入、住房与足够资源的人，才可能实践这个出口。

This helps explain the fantasy’s political ambiguity. A person may genuinely want to leave a damaging workplace, spend more time caring for children, or arrange family life according to religious belief; none of this automatically constitutes antifeminist politics. But when inadequate childcare, low wages, long working hours, and inequality at home are retold as evidence that “women chose the wrong place,” public problems become personal failures. The proposed exit is available mainly to those with a stable partner’s income, housing, and sufficient resources.

### Labour｜劳动

Tradwife 最核心的悖论也在这里：它以逃离工作为承诺，却把家庭生活本身变成了工作。

Here lies the central Tradwife paradox: it promises escape from work by turning domesticity into work.

Sophia Sykes 与 Veronica Hopner 在 2022 年对 36 个 Tradwife 社交媒体账号进行了 10 个月的网络民族志。研究者保存并转录视频、文字和限时内容，结合长期观察寻找重复模式。她们归纳出的四个主题之一，就是“Tradwife side-hustle”。36 个账号中有 35 个存在某种变现方式，包括广告、品牌合作、推广、线上项目或小企业。

In 2022, Sophia Sykes and Veronica Hopner conducted a ten-month netnography of 36 Tradwife social-media accounts. They saved and transcribed video, written, and ephemeral content, combining those materials with sustained observation to identify recurring patterns. One of the four themes they developed was the “Tradwife side-hustle.” Thirty-five of the 36 accounts used some form of monetization, including advertising, brand partnerships, promotions, online programs, or small businesses.

这项研究不代表所有家庭主妇，也不能证明每位 Tradwife 都靠内容获得可观收入。它的样本由研究者从右派 Tradwife 社群中界定，因此适用范围尤其需要注意。但它清楚展示了一件事：镜头中的“不工作”，可能同时包含烹饪、清洁、育儿和情绪维持，也包含选题、拍摄、剪辑、发布、查看数据、维持人设与商业合作。家庭既是生活空间，也是摄影棚；妻子既是照护者，也是表演者、制片人和品牌。

The study does not represent all homemakers, nor does it show that every Tradwife earns substantial income from content. Its sample was selected from what the researchers defined as a right-wing Tradwife community, so its scope requires particular care. It nevertheless demonstrates something important: the “not working” shown on camera may include cooking, cleaning, childcare, and emotional maintenance, as well as planning, filming, editing, posting, monitoring analytics, sustaining a persona, and managing sponsorships. The home becomes both living space and studio; the wife becomes caregiver, performer, producer, and brand.

Caro Claire Burke 的 2026 年小说《Yesteryear》将这组矛盾变成了一场文化思想实验。小说中的 Natalie 同时是妻子、母亲和网红，她精心展示的传统家庭背后有保姆 Louise、制片人 Shannon、农工和藏在镜头外的现代设备。当她进入一个仿佛 1855 年的世界，复古美学失去了现代基础设施，原本可拍摄、可消费的“过去”开始显露为体力、风险和匮乏。

Caro Claire Burke’s 2026 novel *Yesteryear* turns this contradiction into a cultural thought experiment. Natalie, its protagonist, is simultaneously wife, mother, and influencer. Behind her carefully displayed traditional home are Louise the nanny, Shannon the producer, farmworkers, and modern appliances hidden outside the frame. When she enters a world resembling 1855, the vintage aesthetic loses its modern infrastructure, and a “past” designed to be filmed and consumed begins to reveal itself as physical labor, risk, and scarcity.

小说不能证明现实中的 Tradwife 都隐藏团队，也不能替代家庭劳动史。《Yesteryear》的意义在于把一个问题戏剧化：当家庭生活被包装为自然和轻松时，哪些劳动者、技术与资源被移出了画面？

A novel cannot prove that real Tradwives all conceal production teams, nor can it substitute for the history of domestic labor. *Yesteryear* matters because it dramatizes a question: when domestic life is packaged as natural and effortless, which workers, technologies, and resources have been moved outside the frame?

### Choice｜选择

“这是我的选择”是 Tradwife 内容中最有力量、也最难处理的语言。个人选择当然真实存在：有人偏好家庭照护，有人接受宗教分工，有人因托育成本、健康状况或工作环境离开职场。私人安排不应自动受到污名化。

“This is my choice” is one of the most powerful—and most difficult—claims in Tradwife content. Personal choice is real. Some people prefer caregiving at home; some accept a religious division of roles; others leave paid work because of childcare costs, health, or workplace conditions. Private arrangements should not automatically be stigmatized.

但选择并不发生在真空中。收入、住房、伴侣关系、托育价格、法律保障与家庭支持，都会决定哪些选择真正可行。更重要的是，“我选择这样生活”与“女人天生应该这样生活”并非同一主张。前者描述个人安排；后者把一种安排自然化为女性共同的位置。当内容进一步宣扬男性领导、女性服从，或把女性主义描述为社会衰败的来源时，生活方式便转化为规范性政治。

Choice, however, does not occur in a vacuum. Income, housing, partnership, childcare costs, legal protection, and family support all shape which options are realistically available. More importantly, “I choose to live this way” is not the same claim as “women are naturally meant to live this way.” The first describes a private arrangement; the second naturalizes one arrangement as the proper place for women generally. When content goes on to advocate male leadership and female submission, or portrays feminism as the source of social decline, lifestyle becomes normative politics.

Rebecca Stotzer 与 Ashley Nelson 分析了 60 名在 TikTok 上自认 Tradwife 的创作者如何解释自己进入这种生活。研究的价值在于让创作者的自我叙述进入讨论，而不是只由批评者替她们定义动机。但社交媒体自述仍是一种公开表演，不等于完整的生活史，也无法告诉我们这种身份在全国有多普遍。目前没有可靠的全国调查能够估计有多少女性自认 Tradwife。

Rebecca Stotzer and Ashley Nelson analyzed how 60 self-identified Tradwives on TikTok explained why and how they entered the lifestyle. The value of the study is that it brings creators’ own accounts into the discussion instead of allowing critics alone to define their motives. Yet a social-media account remains a public performance rather than a complete life history, and it cannot tell us how prevalent the identity is nationally. There is currently no reliable national survey estimating how many women identify as Tradwives.

### Platform｜平台

Tradwife 幻想看似拒绝现代性，却只有依赖最现代的平台和商业系统才能以今天的形式存在。TikTok、Instagram 与 YouTube 提供分发，推荐系统扩大接触，评论区制造社群，拟社会关系让观众感到自己熟悉屏幕中的家庭；广告、联盟链接、课程、产品和订阅则把这种亲近感变成收入。

The Tradwife fantasy appears to reject modernity, yet in its current form it depends on the most modern platforms and commercial systems. TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube distribute the content; recommendation systems expand exposure; comment sections build community; parasocial relationships make viewers feel they know the family on screen; and advertising, affiliate links, courses, products, and subscriptions convert intimacy into income.

Eviane Leidig 在 2023 年出版的《The Women of the Far Right》中研究的是明确属于极右翼的女性网红。她说明，这些创作者如何把约会、食物、家庭、怀孕和母职等日常内容与白人民族主义、反女权或阴谋论叙事混合。政治不总以演讲或口号出现，而可能借助“真实、亲切、容易模仿”的人设，进入关于择偶、饮食和育儿的日常建议。

In her 2023 book *The Women of the Far Right*, Eviane Leidig studies women influencers who are explicitly part of the far right. She shows how these creators mix everyday content about dating, food, home, pregnancy, and motherhood with white-nationalist, antifeminist, or conspiratorial narratives. Politics does not always arrive as speeches or slogans. Through personas that feel authentic, relatable, and easy to emulate, it can enter ordinary advice about partners, food, and parenting.

Leidig 的研究非常适合解释生活方式内容如何承载政治，却不能被外推为“所有 Tradwife 都是极右翼”。同样，平台能够放大内容、奖励鲜明身份并帮助某些叙事变得日常，并不等于算法单独把观看者“变成”极右翼。接触、认同、态度变化与政治行动是不同层次的问题，现有研究不能把它们压缩成一条自动因果链。

Leidig’s work is highly useful for explaining how lifestyle content can carry politics, but it cannot be generalized into the claim that all Tradwives belong to the far right. Likewise, platforms can amplify content, reward legible identities, and make some narratives feel ordinary without algorithms single-handedly “turning” viewers into extremists. Exposure, identification, attitude change, and political action are different questions; current evidence cannot collapse them into an automatic causal chain.

### Politics｜政治

那么，家庭生活何时成为政治？关键并不是面包、裙子或孩子的数量，而是内容如何解释社会。喜欢烘焙不是政治身份，选择全职照护也不是极右翼证据。可靠判断需要创作者反复表达的主张、自我描述、组织关联，或明确的反女权、民族主义、种族化生育与阴谋论内容。

When, then, does domestic life become political? The answer lies not in bread, dresses, or the number of children, but in how content explains society. Enjoying baking is not a political identity, and choosing full-time caregiving is not evidence of the far right. A responsible judgment requires repeated claims, self-description, organizational ties, or explicit antifeminist, nationalist, racialized pronatalist, or conspiratorial content.

Sykes 与 Hopner 的研究将样本放在右派内部的不同位置，从保守右派到 alt-lite 和 alt-right，没有两个账号完全相同。Pilyarchuk 与 Graf 对 11 个北美社会政治网红账号所做的主题分析与多模态女性主义批判话语分析，则考察了 Tradwife 身份如何通过图像、语言、印象管理和“真实感”被表演。这个探索性小样本适合辨认传播机制，却不能描述所有 Instagram 家庭内容。

Sykes and Hopner located the accounts in their sample across different positions on the right, from conservative right to alt-lite and alt-right, and found no two accounts identical. Pilyarchuk and Graf, in a thematic and multimodal feminist critical discourse analysis of 11 North American sociopolitical influencer accounts, examined how Tradwife identity is performed through image, language, impression management, and claims to authenticity. This exploratory small sample is useful for identifying communication strategies, but it cannot describe all domestic content on Instagram.

这些研究共同提示，Tradwife 不是一个从审美到极端主义整齐排列的单一光谱。更准确的说法是：部分政治化创作者使用家庭美学降低政治语言的进入门槛，把传统性别秩序呈现为常识、疗愈或个人成长；另一些创作者可能只共享视觉符号，并不共享相同意识形态。政治正常化是一种需要警惕的可能性，却不是每次观看都会发生的结果。

Taken together, these studies suggest that Tradwife culture is not a single continuum neatly arranged from aesthetics to extremism. A more precise account is that some politicized creators use domestic aesthetics to lower the threshold for political messages, presenting traditional gender hierarchy as common sense, healing, or personal growth. Other creators may share visual symbols without sharing the same ideology. Political normalization is a possibility worth examining, not an outcome produced by every view.

### Counter-evidence｜反证与复杂性

关于 Tradwife 最容易传播的故事，是一条从 cottagecore 的花朵与田园，经过传统妻子美学，最终通向法西斯主义的“流水线”。但目前最直接检验这一说法的研究，并没有发现如此清晰的自动路径。

One of the most shareable stories about Tradwife culture is a “pipeline” leading from cottagecore flowers and pastoral imagery, through Tradwife aesthetics, and finally to fascism. Yet the study that most directly tested this claim did not find such a clear, automatic route.

Oliver Mel Allen、Yi Zu、Milo Z. Trujillo 与 Brooke Foucault Welles 对超过 20 万条 Tumblr 帖子进行了标签网络与时间分析，并对约 2,350 条帖子进行内容编码。研究没有发现明确的用户激进化流水线。它发现的是更有限的“主流化”风险：cottagecore 与 Tradwife 社群存在重叠，一些传统性别、宗教和家庭标签随时间增加，政治内容也可能在适应平台文化后显得更温和。研究还发现了比预期更多的 queer—Tradwife 互动和反种族主义表达。

Oliver Mel Allen, Yi Zu, Milo Z. Trujillo, and Brooke Foucault Welles conducted tag-network and temporal analyses of more than 200,000 Tumblr posts and content-coded roughly 2,350 posts. They did not find a clear pipeline of user radicalization. What they found was a more limited risk of mainstreaming: cottagecore and Tradwife communities overlapped; traditional-gender, religious, and homemaking tags increased over time; and political content could appear softer after adapting to platform norms. They also found more queer–Tradwife interaction and anti-racist expression than expected.

这项研究只针对 Tumblr，而且没有追踪用户个人的浏览轨迹与态度变化，因此既不能证明激进化，也不能排除其他平台或封闭社群中的路径。但它足以阻止一种过度确定的叙述：搜索鲜花或面包不会自动把人带向法西斯主义。

The study is limited to Tumblr and did not track individual browsing histories or attitude change, so it can neither prove radicalization nor rule out pathways on other platforms or in closed communities. It is nevertheless sufficient to reject an overly deterministic story: searching for flowers or bread does not automatically lead a person to fascism.

标签本身也在被争夺。《纽约客》报道，早期公开使用 Tradwife 身份的 Alena Kate Pettitt 后来退出 Instagram，并与这个标签拉开距离。她认为，它已从一种家庭生活身份变成表演、商业与政治争夺的对象。她仍然重视家庭主妇角色，却不再确定是否愿意继续使用 Tradwife 这个词。2026 年 Hopner 与 Sykes 关于“de-tradding”的研究进一步区分了两种变化：有人拒绝标签，却保留传统家庭生活；有人则拒绝这种生活方式本身。

The label itself is contested. *The New Yorker* reported that Alena Kate Pettitt, an early public user of the Tradwife identity, later left Instagram and distanced herself from the term. She believed it had shifted from a domestic identity into an object of performance, commerce, and political struggle. She continued to value homemaking but was no longer sure she wanted the Tradwife label. Hopner and Sykes’s 2026 study of “de-tradding” further distinguishes between rejecting the label while retaining a traditional domestic life, and rejecting the lifestyle itself.

同样，Nara Smith 曾称自己是 working mom；Hannah Neeleman 也没有可靠证据显示她主动接受 Tradwife 标签。她们的内容可以被分析为如何被观众归类、传播和政治化，却不能据此替她们宣布身份。媒体标签不是自我认同，审美相似也不是政治证据。

Likewise, Nara Smith has described herself as a working mom, while there is no reliable evidence that Hannah Neeleman actively adopts the Tradwife label. Their content can be examined in terms of how audiences classify, circulate, and politicize it, but that does not authorize others to declare their identities for them. A media label is not self-identification, and aesthetic resemblance is not political evidence.

### Past｜过去

Tradwife 幻想所召唤的“过去”，并不是一个完整的历史时期。它更像一幅拼贴：1950 年代消费家庭的厨房、19 世纪先锋生活的自给自足、当代医学的安全、现代电器的便利，再加上社交媒体的滤镜和商业系统。它保留明确角色与漂亮画面，同时删去高强度劳动、法律限制、阶级差异和身体风险。

The “past” summoned by the Tradwife fantasy is not a complete historical period. It is closer to a collage: the consumer kitchen of the 1950s, the self-sufficiency of nineteenth-century pioneer life, the safety of modern medicine, the convenience of contemporary appliances, and the filters and commercial systems of social media. It keeps clear roles and beautiful images while deleting intensive labor, legal constraints, class difference, and bodily risk.

美国人口普查局对 1950 年人口普查的回顾显示，在约 5,700 万劳动年龄女性中，29% 已进入劳动力市场；单身女性为 46.4%，已婚女性为 21.6%。这组数字说明，战后家庭主妇模式确实拥有强大的制度和文化影响，却从未覆盖所有女性。较低的劳动参与率也不能被解读为普遍满意，因为劳动市场排斥、婚姻禁令、照护责任与社会规范都会影响女性是否进入有薪工作。

The U.S. Census Bureau’s review of the 1950 Census shows that 29 percent of roughly 57 million working-age women were in the labor force, including 46.4 percent of single women and 21.6 percent of married women. These figures show that the postwar homemaker model had considerable institutional and cultural power, but never encompassed all women. Lower labor-force participation cannot be read as universal satisfaction: exclusion from employment, marriage bars, care responsibilities, and social norms all shaped women’s access to paid work.

“女性过去不工作”还把工作错误地缩减为领取工资。取水、取燃料、烹饪、制衣、储藏食物、洗衣、育儿和照护病人，不会因为没有工资而消失。史密森尼关于 19 世纪洗衣的资料记录了收集水和燃料、手洗、手拧、晾晒以及用炉火加热重型熨斗的过程。现代复古家庭之所以可以舒适地重演，很大程度上依赖室内供水、电力、冷藏、洗衣设备、食品工业、交通和现代医疗。

The claim that “women did not work in the past” also mistakenly reduces work to wages. Fetching water and fuel, cooking, sewing, preserving food, washing clothes, raising children, and caring for the sick did not disappear because no wage was paid. Smithsonian materials on nineteenth-century laundry describe collecting water and fuel, washing and wringing by hand, drying, and heating heavy irons on a stove. The comfortable reenactment of vintage domesticity today depends substantially on indoor plumbing, electricity, refrigeration, washing machines, industrial food systems, transportation, and modern medicine.

历史家庭理想也有阶级条件。单一男性收入、土地、宽敞厨房、现代设备、居家教育和大量自制食物都需要资源；一些中上阶层家庭还依赖女佣、洗衣工、农工和其他被家庭肖像排除的劳动者。到 1900 年，非裔美国女性已构成美国国内服务劳动者中数量庞大且不断增长的一部分。所谓“妻子独自经营家庭”的怀旧画面，经常把阶级化和种族化的辅助劳动移到镜头之外。

The historical domestic ideal was also conditioned by class. A single male income, land, a large kitchen, modern equipment, homeschooling, and extensive food preparation all require resources. Some middle- and upper-class households also relied on domestic servants, laundresses, farmworkers, and others excluded from the family portrait. By 1900, African American women made up a large and growing share of domestic-service workers in the United States. Nostalgic images of “the wife running the home alone” often move classed and racialized support labor outside the frame.

生育风险同样不能被田园美学抹去。美国疾病控制与预防中心的历史资料显示，20 世纪初美国每 1,000 次活产约有 6 至 9 名女性死于妊娠相关并发症；到 1997 年，这一数字降至每 10 万次活产 7.7 人。这不是 1855 年的精确数据，也不是今天的孕产妇死亡率。它能说明的只是：营养、临床医学、医疗可及性、公共卫生监测与生活条件，深刻改变了“传统生育”的物质风险。

Nor can pastoral aesthetics erase the risks of reproduction. Historical material from the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention reports that, in the early twentieth century, approximately six to nine women died from pregnancy-related complications for every 1,000 live births in the United States. By 1997, the figure had fallen to 7.7 per 100,000 live births. This is neither a precise estimate for 1855 nor the current maternal mortality rate. What it shows is that nutrition, clinical medicine, access to care, public-health surveillance, and living conditions profoundly changed the material risks of “traditional” reproduction.

### What the fantasy sells｜幻想出售的是什么

Tradwife 幻想之所以迷人，不是因为观众都想回到一个真实存在过的年代，而是因为它把几种当代渴望压缩成一幅容易理解的画面：从过劳中休息，让照护重新获得意义，摆脱持续竞争，拥有稳定的家庭和可预测的身份。它让结构性问题看起来可以通过个人生活方式解决。

The Tradwife fantasy is seductive not because every viewer wants to return to a period that actually existed, but because it compresses several contemporary desires into a legible image: rest from overwork, renewed meaning for care, escape from permanent competition, a stable family, and a predictable identity. It makes structural problems appear solvable through personal lifestyle.

但这个出口伴随代价。它可能隐藏无偿劳动与内容劳动，依赖伴侣收入和阶级资源，把照护危机重新私有化，并在部分政治化版本中，把女性主义塑造成问题本身。与此同时，证据也不支持把所有家庭照护、复古美学或 Tradwife 内容归入同一种政治。审美可以承载意识形态，却不自动等于意识形态；平台可以促进正常化，却没有被证明会自动制造激进化。

But the exit carries costs. It can conceal unpaid labor and content labor, depend on a partner’s income and class resources, reprivatize failures of care, and—in some politicized versions—cast feminism as the problem itself. At the same time, the evidence does not support placing all homemaking, vintage aesthetics, or Tradwife content in one political category. Aesthetics can carry ideology without automatically being ideology; platforms can facilitate normalization without having been shown to automatically produce radicalization.

因此，目前最可辩护的结论不是“Tradwife 是自由”或“Tradwife 是极右翼”，而是：**Tradwife 文化可以把结构性的疲惫转化成一场个人化的回归幻想；但这场幻想在政治上并不统一，也不会自动使人激进化。**真正值得追问的，不是一个女人是否烤面包、穿长裙或选择留在家中，而是谁承担照护，谁拥有经济保障，哪些劳动被看见，以及个人选择何时被包装成所有女性都应服从的秩序。

The most defensible conclusion, then, is neither “Tradwife is freedom” nor “Tradwife is the far right.” It is this: **Tradwife culture can turn structural exhaustion into an individualized fantasy of return, yet the fantasy is neither politically uniform nor automatically radicalizing.** The questions that matter are not whether a woman bakes bread, wears a long dress, or chooses to stay home. They are who performs care, who has economic security, which forms of labor remain visible, and when a personal arrangement is repackaged as an order all women are expected to obey.
